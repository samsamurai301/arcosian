## Slide plan
| # | Slide | Must answer | Evidence to show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title and one-line pitch | What is it, in one breath? | Logo, key art |
| 2 | Hook / player fantasy | Why would a player care? | Gameplay GIF or 30 s video |
| 3 | Core loop | What do they do minute to minute? | Loop diagram, timed beats |
| 4 | Differentiator | Why this, not the 50 similar games? | Side-by-side with 2-3 comparables |
| 5 | Playable / build status | What exists today? | Build link, screenshots, video |
| 6 | Audience and market | Who plays, how large, how reachable? | Cited market data (volatile, dated) |
| 7 | Retention and progression | Why do they return? | Systems summary, early cohort data if any |
| 8 | Monetization | How does it earn, ethically? | Model, price points logic, projected ARPDAU labeled assumption |
| 9 | Data so far | Is there signal? | Playtests, soft-launch metrics vs gates |
| 10 | Roadmap and live ops | What ships when? | Milestones, calendar |
| 11 | Team | Why can you deliver? | Shipped titles, roles, gaps |
| 12 | Budget and ask | What do you need and for what? | Use of funds by milestone, runway |
| 13 | Risks and mitigations | What could go wrong? | Top 3 with tests |
| 14 | Contact and next step | What should they do now? | One concrete ask |

## Narrative checks
- The first three slides should make a stranger want to see slide 5.
- Show gameplay early; text-heavy slides lose the room.
- One idea per slide; at most [30] words of body text.
- Be honest about weaknesses; reviewers find them anyway and trust you more if you did first.

## Q and A prep
- Why will players choose this over [comparable]?
- What is your acquisition plan and cost assumption?
- What happens if retention is below plan?
- Who owns the IP and the tools licences? Any AI-generated content, and how is it handled?
- What is the smallest version you would ship if funding is half?
