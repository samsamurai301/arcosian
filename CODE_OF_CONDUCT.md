# Code of conduct

Arcosian adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good faith, give and take criticism about the work rather than the person, and do not harass, insult or publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer:

- through this repository's private reporting form, <https://github.com/samsamurai301/arcosian/security/advisories/new>, with a title that starts with "Conduct:" (only the maintainer can read it), or
- with GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue or pull request.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban) and may remove content that breaks this code.
